Kalau lu tanya apa yang paling gua syukuri dari delapan bulan ini, jawabannya simple: gua ketemu lu.

a little more than eight months

If I could
ask for one thing.

I wouldn't ask for a perfect relationship. I wouldn't ask for every single day to be easy. I wouldn't ask us to never fight, never misunderstand each other, or never have bad days.

Gua cuma mau kita tetap punya alasan buat balik satu sama lain. Even after the bad days. Even after the awkward conversations. Even after everything changes around us.

Karena gua sadar, people change, situations change, everything around us can change. But I hope one thing doesn't: the fact that we still choose each other.

So, Abas, kalau suatu hari nanti lu baca ini lagi setelah kita udah jauh lebih lama dari delapan bulan, gua harap lu masih bisa senyum dan inget bahwa this was written when I was genuinely happy to have you.

And if life lets me be selfish, gua pengen kenal lu bukan cuma untuk eight months. I want more random conversations. More stupid jokes. More good mornings. More good nights. More moments where we don't even realize how much time has passed.

Basically, gua mau lu hidup lama banget. Kalau bisa, forever. Dan kalau boleh, tetap hidupnya sama gua.
